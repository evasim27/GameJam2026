Tool: built-in `image_gen`, transparent background enabled.

Use case: style-transfer
Asset type: single transparent pixel-art game enemy variant for Lost in Tape.
Reimagine the Tape Monster from reference 1 with approximately the body shape of the Stranger Things Demogorgon: tall lean humanoid, hunched shoulders, very long arms with claw-like fingers, bent digitigrade legs, and an open five-petaled predatory flower head. This is a VHS TAPE creature: ALL anatomy formed from wound and interwoven dark magnetic tape ribbons, not skin. Five broad curling tape loops form the head petals, with small ivory broken-plastic teeth along their inner edges surrounding a deep black circular maw. Small cassette reel components embedded in the chest connect it to the tape monster identity. Loose short tape ends hang from elbows and back. No normal face or eyes on the flower head.
References 2 and 3 provide the art style: COARSE readable pixel art, bold dark outlines, broad square pixel clusters, limited 3-step shading. Match pumpkin and skeleton simplicity. Near-black plum outlines, dark brown magnetic tape with restrained amber highlights, ivory teeth. No realistic flesh, no blood, no gore.
One full-body creature in a threatening idle pose, slight three-quarter side view facing RIGHT, head flower open and visible, long arms held away from torso so hands read separately, both feet grounded on same invisible baseline. Entire silhouette fits comfortably on portrait canvas with generous transparent margin at all edges. Sprite about 80 logical art pixels tall, intentionally chunky and readable at game scale, no tiny texture, no dithering, no gradients, no blur or antialiasing. Exactly ONE sprite, no grid or extra poses, no scenery, no floor or shadow, no text, no watermark. True transparent background.
